# Quizzes & mock exams

Quizzes turn your materials into deliberate practice. Mocks add exam conditions — timers, mixed question types, and structured review.

## Create a quiz

You can generate from:

- **Chat** — describe count, types, timer, and difficulty
- **Quizzes** pages — new quiz flows and settings sheets
- **Study builder** — edit questions after generation

Common settings include timer minutes, shuffle questions/options, option counts, and question types (MCQ, short answer, true/false, matching, and mixed mocks).

## Exam simulation journey

1. Select course materials and scope.
2. Configure timer, question mix, and difficulty.
3. Take the mock under exam conditions.
4. Review score, explanations, and weak topics.
5. See what is worth revisiting next.

## Taking and reviewing

- Play quizzes in the quiz player with optional timing.
- Submit to see score and per-question feedback.
- The review screen shows your answer, the correct answer (for objective questions), explanations, and source material names.
- Use pagination and the **Incorrect only** filter to focus on misses.
- Use review to understand *why* an answer missed the mark — especially when a Professor Agent is attached.
- Misses can feed [flashcards](./flashcards) and [progress](./progress).

## Editing

Generated quizzes can be edited before or after sharing with a group. Prefer fixing distractors and mark schemes early so shared practice stays trustworthy.
