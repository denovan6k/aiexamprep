# Billing & plans

Knorvex uses subscription plans and usage entitlements so free users can try the product while Pro unlocks more regular practice.

## Plans (overview)

- **Free** — a few courses and uploads to get started
- **Student Pro** — more room for regular quizzes, generation, and study workflows
- **Team / Enterprise** — cohort and institutional options as they roll out

Exact limits and prices are shown on the in-app [Pricing](https://knorvex.com/pricing) page and may change; treat the app as the source of truth for current entitlements.

## Managing billing

1. Open **Settings → Billing** (or the billing page in the account menu).
2. Review your current plan and open Stripe checkout or the customer portal when prompted.
3. For charts and quota meters, open **Usage** in the workspace sidebar.

## Usage page

The **Usage** page shows:

- Daily activity trends (chat, uploads, courses, agents, CV tailor, and other events)
- Breakdown by event type
- Current-period quota meters against your plan limits

Pick a 7 / 30 / 90 day range to explore recent history. Upgrade or buy credits from Billing when you hit a cap.

## Credits and usage

Some generation and AI features consume plan credits or rate limits. **Settings → Billing** and **Usage** both show live charts and quota meters from your account events. If a generation is blocked, check those pages, wait for the period to reset, or upgrade if you have outgrown the free tier.

## Support

Billing issues can be raised through support channels in the app. Keep invoices and Stripe emails for your records.
