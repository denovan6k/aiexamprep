# Progress & study plans

Progress in Knorvex is meant to change what you do next — not to celebrate empty streaks.

## After each attempt

You can see:

- What is landing
- What still needs work
- Where the next study session will have the most impact

Use the **Today** dashboard and **Progress** views to pick the next quiz, deck, or mock instead of rereading chapters you already know.

## Weak topics

Weak-topic tracking highlights areas that keep showing up in misses. Drill those with targeted quizzes or flashcards before finals week.

## Study plans

Study plans group upcoming practice so exam season stays deliberate. Prefer short, timed blocks over marathon rereading.
