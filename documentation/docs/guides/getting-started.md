# Getting started

Your notes already know this course. Knorvex helps them test you like the exam will.

## First session

1. **Sign up** with email and password, then verify your email if prompted.
2. **Create a course** for the subject you are revising.
3. **Upload** lecture notes, a textbook chapter, or a past paper (PDF, DOCX, plain text, or Markdown).
4. Wait while Knorvex organises the material for practice.
5. **Create a quiz or flashcard set** from that course — from chat or the Quizzes / Flashcards pages.
6. **Review what you missed** and keep going.

## What you can practise

- Flashcards
- Multiple-choice and multi-select questions
- Short-answer theory
- Long-form essays
- Mixed mock exams
- Oral exam simulations
- Presentation defences

## Tips

- Start with one strong PDF or set of notes rather than dumping everything at once.
- Save a [Professor Agent](./professor-agents) early if you know how your lecturer writes questions.
- Use [Progress](./progress) after a few attempts so the next study block targets weak topics.
