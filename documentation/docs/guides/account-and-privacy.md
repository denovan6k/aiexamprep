# Account & privacy

## Sign-in

Create an account with email and password. Verify your email when asked. Password reset and email verification flows live under the auth pages (`/sign-in`, `/forgot-password`, `/email-verification`).

Additional OAuth providers may appear over time; email/password is the baseline.

## Settings

From **Settings** you can review your profile, update study preferences (daily minutes and study goal), open [billing](./billing), and manage generation automation and support tickets.

**Models & API keys** (`/settings/models`) is where you:

- Browse the live platform model catalog (search, provider filter, free/reasoning chips, pagination)
- Set task defaults for chat, generation, and embeddings
- Save bring-your-own OpenAI or Anthropic keys for chat

Signed-out visitors can reach the team via the public [Contact](https://knorvex.com/contact) form.

## Your data

- Course materials and chat stay in your account unless you share derived resources.
- Community sharing never exposes raw uploads by default — see [Community](./community).
- For GDPR rights, cookies, and terms, use the legal pages in the app (kept authoritative there, not duplicated here).
