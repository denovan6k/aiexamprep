# Knorvex documentation

**Stop rereading. Start getting tested.**

Knorvex is an agentic exam-prep product. You upload the material you already have, pick a professor-style agent, and generate practice that points at what still needs work — quizzes, flashcards, timed mocks, and study groups.

These docs cover:

- **[Guides](./guides/getting-started)** — how students use Knorvex day to day
- **[Developers](./developers/architecture)** — how the product is structured

## Product promise

> Turn the notes you have into practice that moves you forward.

Practice is built from *your* notes, slides, textbooks, and past papers — not generic chatbot drills. After each attempt you see weak topics and a clearer next step.

## Design principles

1. **Material-first** — practice comes from what you uploaded.
2. **Professor-style agents** — exam tone and marking without becoming a chatbot playground.
3. **Progress that changes behavior** — weak topics drive the next session, not vanity streaks.
4. **Calm exam prep** — deliberate practice under realistic conditions.
5. **Privacy-aware sharing** — share quizzes, decks, and agents; raw uploads stay private unless you choose otherwise.
